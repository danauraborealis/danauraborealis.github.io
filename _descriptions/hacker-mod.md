The Mod
==============

This mod adds two new devices:

Hacker Device (2/2 Charges)
Ref's Hacker Device (4/4 Charges)

Both devices allow you to complete a mini-game. Successful completion will allow you to unlock Keycard doors, and hack ATM machines for a bit of cash. 

The basic device can be crafted in your hideout, while Ref's device needs to be earned by completing a Unique quest for him.

Ref's device will allow you to complete the hack without using up a charge, and earn extra cash if hacking an ATM.
