The Mod
==============
This mod adds Combine Overwatch soldiers from Half-Life 2. 
They come with a custom armor vest, a custom helmet, and a custom voice.

The voice was created by me using HL2 files. The assets are from Nemez Combine Soldier Pack and converted by me to work in EFT.
 
This mod was created using WTT - Black Division [REDACTED] Home as an example and works similarly to that mod, including a config to control spawns.


WARNING
---------------
They have top tier armor and weapons and are very difficult to fight. Good luck!
